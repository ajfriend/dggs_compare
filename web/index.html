Discrete global grid systems — H3, S2, A5, ISEA7H, IVEA7H, ISEA3H, IVEA3H, ISEA4T, rHEALPix, hex9 — compared by per-cell shape. Aspect ratio (AR) is the major/minor axis ratio of a cell's tightest enclosing-cone ellipse (the per-cell analogue of Tissot's indicatrix): AR = 1 is isotropic, AR > 1 is squished. Since these grids are ~equal-area, AR is where the unavoidable distortion surfaces.

Methodology: every cell is measured as a spherical polygon — vertices on the unit sphere, great-circle edges. Systems declared on an ellipsoid are brought to the sphere by the area-preserving authalic latitude, so a system that is exactly equal-area on its declared surface measures exactly equal-area here. Details in #42.

Globes — cells colored by aspect ratio

One globe per system at its coarsest full-coverage resolution, colored on a shared AR scale so the systems compare directly. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, hover for a cell's id and AR. The Colormap menu picks the colors; the Scaling menu picks where along the AR axis they are spent — hold one fixed and switch the other (e.g. the stretched default vs. linear on the same colormap) to see how the choice changes what's visible. Globes and legend update together.
