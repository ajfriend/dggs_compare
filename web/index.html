Discrete global grid systems — H3, S2, A5, ISEA7H, IVEA7H, ISEA3H, IVEA3H, ISEA4T, rHEALPix, hex9 — compared by per-cell shape. Aspect ratio (AR) is the major/minor axis ratio of a cell's tightest enclosing-cone ellipse (the per-cell analogue of Tissot's indicatrix): AR = 1 is isotropic, AR > 1 is squished. Since these grids are ~equal-area, AR is where the unavoidable distortion surfaces.

Authalicity — cell-area uniformity

Each cell's area as a ratio to the ideal equal-area share (4π/N at the working resolution) — 1.0 is perfect, and the spread is the areal counterpart of the AR story. Shared log bins; note the ~100× difference in spread between systems. Areas are measured on the common coordinate-sphere datum; systems that internally address the WGS84 authalic sphere or ellipsoid also get a native-datum CV in the survey output.

Globes — cells colored by shape or area

One globe per system at its coarsest full-coverage resolution, colored on a shared scale so the systems compare directly. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, hover for a cell's id, AR and area ratio. The Metric menu picks what is colored — aspect ratio (shape) or area ratio (each cell's area against the ideal equal-area share, 1.0 = perfect); the Colormap menu picks the colors; the Scaling menu picks where along the value axis they are spent — hold two fixed and switch the third to see how the choice changes what's visible. Globes and legend update together.
